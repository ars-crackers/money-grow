Income structure
01

Income & Typesவருமானம் & வகைகள்

Money reaches you in three ways. Most people only have the first — the goal is to add the other two.

What is income? Income is simply the money that comes to you — what you earn from work or receive from what you own. Where it comes from is what decides how free you are with money.
Three streams into one wallet
A

Active income · you work for it

Trade time and effort for money. It stops the moment you stop — and it's taxed the most.

ExamplesSalaryFreelance workDaily wages
P

Portfolio income · investments grow

Money you invested earns more money through price growth, dividends or interest.

ExamplesMutual fund / SIP gainsShare dividendsFD interest
P

Passive income · assets pay you

An asset keeps paying with little ongoing work — income while you sleep.

ExamplesHouse rentBusiness that runs itselfRoyalties
How it links · to the quadrant

Active is the E · S side (you work for money). Portfolio and passive are the B · I side (money works for you). Growing wealth means slowly building the second and third streams.

📕 From Rich Dad's Cashflow Quadrant · Robert Kiyosaki

There are only 4 ways money comes to you. The letters E-S-B-I show where your income comes from.

Left (E · S)You work for money — active income. Stop working, income stops.

Your money earns more money. This is the asset column from the last chapter.

Conventional examples Mutual funds / SIPRental propertyDividendsFD / SGB interest
The goal · Rich Dad's Cashflow Quadrant

Move from the left to the right. You don't have to quit your job — start small on the I side with a monthly SIP, so money begins working for you alongside your salary.

Two ways to hold money
04

Saving & Investmentசேமிப்பு & முதலீடு

Saving keeps money safe and ready; investing puts it to work. You need both, in order.

Park it safely · then let it grow

Saving சேமிப்பு

+

Money set aside for safety and quick access.

Low risk, low return. Build an emergency fund of 6 months' expenses here before investing a single rupee — so you never have to sell investments at a bad time.
Safety first

Investment முதலீடு

+

Money put to work for growth.

Accepts some risk for higher return over a long horizon. This is where compounding does its heavy lifting. Save first, then invest the surplus.
Growth engine
Lesson · Rich Dad Poor Dad

Assets vs liabilities — judged by cashflow. An asset puts cashflow into your pocket; a liability takes it out. Fund your assets before expenses — pay yourself first.

Saving & Investment typesவகைகள்

The instruments, with typical long-term Indian returns. Tap + for where each fits.

From safe coins to higher-growth instruments

Savings & FD / RD / PPF

+

The safe column — protect, don't grow.

Savings a/c ~3% (fully liquid) · FD/RD ~7% (locked, guaranteed) · PPF ~7.1% (tax-free, 15 yr). Ideal for emergency funds and short goals.
~3–7% p.a.

Gold / SGB & Real estate

+

Inflation hedges, slower to sell.

Gold / Sovereign Gold Bonds ~8–10% (SGB adds interest on top of price). Real estate ~8–10% but illiquid and lumpy. Good diversifiers, not your only engine.
~8–10% p.a.

Mutual funds / SIP & Equity

+

The growth engine — highest long-run return.

SIP / mutual funds ~10–12% (a monthly SIP automates compounding). Index funds & equity ~12%+ — highest growth, highest swings, needs a long horizon.
~10–12%+ p.a.
Lesson · Rich Dad Poor Dad

Build your asset column → escape the rat race. When cashflow from assets covers your monthly expenses, you no longer depend on a paycheck. That point is financial freedom.

The two forces
07

Compounding & Inflationகூட்டு வளர்ச்சி & பணவீக்கம்

One force quietly grows your money; the other quietly shrinks it. Tap + to open each.

Same money, two directions — over time

Compounding effect கூட்டு வளர்ச்சி

+

Returns that earn their own returns.

Time is the biggest lever — starting early beats investing more later. Small, regular amounts snowball: ₹5,000/month at 12% for 20 years ≈ ₹50 lakh, of which most is growth, not your deposits.
Your friend ↑

Inflation பணவீக்கம்

+

The yearly rise in prices, ~6% in India.

₹100 today buys less in 10 years. Money left idle loses value silently. Real return = your return − inflation — a 7% FD against 6% inflation is only 1% of real growth.
Your tax ↓
Lesson · Rich Dad Poor Dad

Make money work for you. Most people work for money for life; the shift is building money that earns while you sleep — exactly the compounding cashflow above.

Give money a job
08

Beat inflationபணவீக்கத்தை வெல்லுங்கள்

Idle money leaks ~6% a year to inflation. So give every rupee a job — in this order.

Climb each step to stay above inflation
1

Emergency fund first

6 months of expenses in an FD or liquid fund, so you never break your investments in a crisis.

2

Clear costly debt

Credit cards and personal loans cost 15–40% — no investment beats that. Kill these before investing.

3

Protect with insurance

Health + term cover, so one hospital bill or setback can't wipe out your savings.

4

Invest above inflation

Put the surplus to work in SIP / index funds and PPF, targeting returns above ~6% so money grows in real terms.

5

Diversify & review

Spread across equity, gold and debt. Check once a year and raise your SIP whenever your income rises.

The habit that makes it work

Pay yourself first, and automate it. On salary day, money moves to savings and SIP before you spend. What you don't see, you don't spend — and your money stays ahead of inflation.
